## Scope

Home page `/` (one long page of chapters) plus `/proyectos/[slug]` case studies. Visitor mode: Experience (portfolio) with a Persuade duty: CV, projects and contact must be obvious within 5 seconds.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio is the first volume of a light novel from Pasto. It refuses the category default of headline + subcopy + card grid: the cover is a real LN cover (title block, summoning-circle portrait, vertical volume spine, and a vermilion obi carrying the blurb and actions).

OWN-WORLD: Washi paper and ink, Galeras night; barniz de Pasto colors (vermilion obi, jade, gold, turquoise) as cut flat layers on dark wood for inserts. Mincho display (Shippori Mincho B1), Zen Kaku Gothic body, JetBrains Mono folios. Ruby annotations, folios, dotted leaders, generated rune sigils.

STORY: Visitor learns who he is (name, role, studying at UCC, from Pasto), sees the proof (volumes = projects with GitHub + demo), trusts it (letters, chronicle) and writes (Continuará…).

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left 7 cols: mono eyebrow, two-line H1 at display scale, role, status pill + origin chip. Right 5 cols: portrait at the center of a giant WebGL summoning circle (static SVG fallback). Right edge: vertical 巻一 spine. Bottom: Galeras cut-layer silhouette standing on the full-width vermilion obi with the welcome line and Descargar CV / Ver proyectos / Contactar.

FORM: Brief-pinned direction (light-novel volume), not rolled. seed: none (brief-pinned). Signature interaction: save-point rail with rewind vs page-turn; motion grammar: slow counter-rotation, ease-book cubic-bezier(0.22,1,0.36,1).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
